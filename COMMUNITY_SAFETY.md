# Community and Table-Finder Safety Model

The current repository implements the public discovery interface and data model only. It does not claim that identity verification, private messaging, moderation, or background checks are live.

## Definition of done for the secure community milestone

- authenticated accounts with verified email
- optional stronger identity verification with a documented provider
- private in-platform contact requests
- block and report controls
- moderation queue with evidence and audit history
- code-of-conduct acceptance and version tracking
- age-band controls
- guardian or organization-managed participation for minors
- coarse public location only
- no public email address, phone number, or home address
- accessibility and content-note fields
- host approval levels with expiration and revocation
- abuse-rate monitoring and incident response procedures
- data retention and deletion policy

## Trust levels

Trust badges describe completed platform checks, not personal character or guaranteed safety.

- New: email verified and code of conduct accepted.
- Reviewed: profile and identity signals checked.
- Table-tested: completed sessions and references without unresolved reports.
- Community host: approved to organize public or youth-safe events.

## Data minimization

Public listings may show city or region, time zone, schedule, system, role, age band, format, accessibility, experience level, and content notes.

The platform must not expose precise addresses until an approved event flow requires them. Home addresses should never become generally visible.

## Moderation principles

- prioritize immediate blocking and user safety
- preserve relevant evidence with limited access
- distinguish interpersonal mismatch from harassment or danger
- document badge removal and account sanctions
- give users a way to appeal moderation decisions
- involve emergency services only when a credible immediate threat requires it
