# Third-Party Rules and Trademark Register

This register controls what may be copied, adapted, summarized, or named in the Find Your Table and Dungeon Cards codebase.

It is a production checklist, not legal advice. Recheck the controlling license before each commercial release.

## D&D fifth edition — 2014 baseline

- Rules source: System Reference Document 5.1.
- Preferred license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
- Permitted: SRD 5.1 text and mechanics with the exact attribution required by its legal preamble.
- Excluded unless separately licensed: non-SRD books, official settings, characters, art, logos, maps, and trade dress.
- Required attribution:

> This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 (“SRD 5.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

## Pathfinder Second Edition Remaster

- Rules path: material designated as Licensed Material in ORC-licensed Paizo publications.
- ORC does not license Paizo trademarks, setting content, named characters, artwork, maps, logos, or trade dress.
- Keep every ORC-derived Pathfinder section separable from unrelated systems.
- Include the required ORC notice and identify the licensed source material actually used.
- Do not use a Pathfinder compatibility logo or proprietary font unless every requirement of the current Paizo Compatibility License is met.
- A free website and a paid product can have different trademark-license consequences. Recheck before monetization.

## Basic Roleplaying Universal Game Engine

- Rules path: Chaosium’s BRP Universal Game Engine ORC Content Document.
- Permitted: Licensed Material under ORC with the required ORC and source notices.
- Excluded: Chaosium trademarks including Call of Cthulhu, product identity, art, graphic design, and trade dress.

## Call of Cthulhu Seventh Edition

- No general open license permits copying the Call of Cthulhu rules brand and protected expressions.
- Website treatment: independent commentary, review, and high-level original teaching summaries only.
- Do not reproduce rulebook text, tables, spells, Mythos entities as expressed by Chaosium, scenarios, character sheets, art, maps, logos, or trade dress.
- Commercial Call of Cthulhu material requires an applicable Chaosium path such as Miskatonic Repository, a limited license, or a commercial license.
- Public-domain Mythos fiction does not make Chaosium’s game text public domain.

## Repository-wide rules

1. Use original prose unless a specific licensed passage is necessary.
2. Store system ID, edition, source, license version, attribution, and last-reviewed date with every rules module.
3. Use original art and a visual identity that cannot be mistaken for an official publisher product.
4. Never imply publisher approval, sponsorship, certification, or official status.
5. Run a pre-release audit covering text, names, art, metadata, advertising, screenshots, downloadable files, and distribution channel.
